# Code of Conduct

Quant Finance AGH aims to provide a respectful environment for learning, research, and engineering, including people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## How we work together

- Discuss ideas constructively and support claims with evidence.
- Respect collaborators' time, communicate blockers, and give useful feedback.
- Share knowledge and welcome questions from people who are still learning.
- Respect copyright, privacy, confidentiality, and data-use restrictions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, spam, unauthorized advertising, impersonation, and disclosure of private information without authorization are unacceptable.

English is preferred for shared documentation and research contributions. Both English and Polish are welcome in everyday discussions. Use English when needed to include international collaborators.

## Reporting a concern

Contact a Board member privately through the society's existing Discord. If the concern involves that person, contact another Board member or an organization owner who is not involved. Do not post private details or allegations in a public issue.

The people handling a report should listen to those involved, protect information from unnecessary disclosure, and agree on a proportionate response. A response may include a request to change behavior, moderation of content, or a review of collaboration access by the authorized owners. Anyone with a conflict of interest should step aside from handling the report.

These community guidelines supplement the society's official regulations. They do not replace formal membership or organizational decision procedures.
